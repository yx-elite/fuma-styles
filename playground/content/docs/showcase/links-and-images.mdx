## Footnotes

Caches trade freshness for speed[^1]. DNS answers are cached at several levels[^dns], each with its own expiry.

[^1]: A stale entry is one whose original has changed since it was cached.

[^dns]: The browser, the operating system and the resolver can all hold a copy.
